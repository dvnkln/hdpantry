# Roadmap

Ideas for later – in no fixed order and without dates. Nothing here is built yet.

- [ ] **Food groups** – group similar foods and see at a glance what is in stock.
- [ ] **Print your own QR labels** – for containers that come without a code.
- [ ] **Notifications via Pushover** – the same reminders through [Pushover](https://pushover.net), for devices that cannot receive push messages from the app. Off unless you set it up.
- [ ] **NFC** – tap a tag instead of scanning a code.
